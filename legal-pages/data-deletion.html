Data Deletion Policy

Deleting your data

Darija to English provides in-app controls for consent withdrawal and account deletion.

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Default Retention

Original recordings are stored temporarily to complete processing and are deleted from app storage after worker processing. Unsaved results expire automatically. Saved history is opt-in.

Saved History

If you save a result, saved history may include transcripts, translations, generated audio, filenames, timestamps, and job metadata. Deleting your account removes saved output history tied to your account from active app storage.

Withdraw AI Consent

Use Account > Withdraw AI consent to block future AI processing. Withdrawing consent does not automatically delete saved history or your account.

Delete Account

Use Account > Delete account to delete your Clerk account and remove or anonymize app data tied to your account. Some minimal anonymized consent/audit evidence, provider logs, security records, and backups may remain for limited operational or legal reasons.

Support

If you cannot access your account, contact support from the Support page and include the email address associated with your account.
