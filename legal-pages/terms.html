Rules for using Darija to English

Use the app only if you accept these terms and the Privacy Policy.

Effective date: June 2, 2026

Service

Darija to English provides AI-assisted transcription, translation, and speech generation for Moroccan Darija and English voice workflows.

No High-Stakes Use

AI outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, mistranslated, or unnatural. Do not use the app for legal, medical, emergency, immigration, financial, safety-critical, or professional decisions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Only upload or record audio that you have the right to process.
  • Do not use the service to violate laws, impersonate others, harass anyone, or process content you are not authorized to share.
  • Review generated text and audio before relying on or sharing it.

AI Processing Consent

You must accept current AI processing consent before recording, uploading, translating, or generating audio. You can withdraw consent from the Account screen, which blocks future AI processing until you consent again.

Availability

The app may be unavailable or may produce failed jobs because it depends on network connectivity and third-party providers.

Account Deletion

If you create an account, you can initiate account deletion from the Account screen. Deletion removes or anonymizes app data tied to your account, subject to limited audit, security, backup, and legal retention.
